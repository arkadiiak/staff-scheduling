# Staff Scheduling System — Main Location

## Overview
Monthly staff rota and shift planning system for a team of **22 employees** at the main salon location. Covers nail masters, brow masters, administrators, and support staff across a full calendar month.

Scheduling is managed across two systems — Zenoti (booking platform) for individual shift logging, and a master Excel rota for planning, coverage analysis, and cross-location admin coordination.

---

## The Problem This Solves
With 22 staff members on flexible schedules, effective rota management means:
- Maintaining **minimum daily coverage** (target: 7+ nail masters, 2 brow masters per day)
- Identifying **understaffed days in advance** — not on the day itself
- Spotting **overloaded days** to avoid service delays and client complaints
- Ensuring **every location has confirmed admin cover** each day across 6 sites

---

## My Role in Schedule Management
A core part of my daily responsibilities is **actively managing and adjusting the rota** throughout the month:
- Monitoring the daily staff count against client bookings and forecasted demand
- **Replacing absent masters** at short notice — contacting on-call staff or redistributing bookings
- Adjusting the schedule when masters request days off, go on leave, or are unavailable
- Ensuring admin cover is confirmed across all 6 locations each day
- Flagging days with insufficient brow master coverage and finding solutions

This is not a static document — it is a live operational tool updated daily.

---

## What's Inside (3 Sheets)

### Sheet 1 — Main Location Monthly Rota
Full June 2026 view for all 22 employees:

| Code | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| W | Working shift (confirmed) |
| V | Vacation / Annual Leave |
| ! | Unplanned absence or flagged issue |
| (blank) | Scheduled day off |

Colour-coded by role for quick visual scan:
- 🔵 Manager / Top Nail Masters / Nail Masters
- 🟢 Brow Masters
- 🟡 Administrator
- ⚫ Cleaners

**Daily count rows at the bottom** show nail and brow master numbers per day:
- 🟢 Green = Strong (9–10 nail, 2 brow)
- 🟡 Yellow = Acceptable (7–8 nail, 1 brow)
- 🔴 Red = Below minimum

### Sheet 2 — Admin Rota (All Locations)
Daily admin and manager coverage across all 6 locations. Status codes:
- 🔴 **Red** = Away / Cannot attend / Cancelled
- 🟡 **Orange** = N/A / Unconfirmed / TBC
- White = Confirmed cover

### Sheet 3 — Coverage Analysis
Weekly staffing summary with averages, min/max per day, and a log of schedule adjustments made during the month.
